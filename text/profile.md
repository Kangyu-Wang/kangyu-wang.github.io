Hi! I am a philosopher, working as an Assistant Lecturer at [the Department of Philosophy](https://philosophy.hku.hk), [the Faculty of Arts](https://arts.hku.hk), [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk). Alongside research and teaching, I work on developing and coordinating HKU's new [5-year BA & BEng in AI and Data Science double degree programme](https://aidata.arts.hku.hk). I am a Research Affiliate at HKU [AI & Humanities Lab](https://ai-humanity.net).

I earned my [PhD in Philosophy](https://www.lse.ac.uk/study-at-lse/graduate/mphilphd-philosophy) from [The London School of Economics & Political Science (LSE)](https://www.lse.ac.uk), in the [Department of Philosophy, Logic & Scientific Method](https://www.lse.ac.uk/philosophy/), supervised by [Campbell Brown](https://philpeople.org/profiles/campbell-brown) and [Kieran Oberman](https://philpeople.org/profiles/kieran-oberman) and previously by [Johanna Thoma (Bayreuth)](https://johannathoma.com/). My thesis examiners were [Richard Bradley](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/bradleyr/) and [Ruth Chang (Oxford)](https://www.ruthchang.net/contact).

I mainly work on practical reason and decision theory, philosophy of machine learning/artificial intelligence, philosophy of economics and finance, and moral and political philosophy more generally. Above all else, I'm exploring the intersection of decision theory and computer science. For a work I'll present at the cross-disciplinary [ACM FAccT 2026](https://facctconference.org/2026/index.html), see [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15304).

I am a Research Affiliate at [The Machine Intelligence and Normative Theory (MINT) Lab](https://mintresearch.org)（PI: [Seth Lazar](https://sethlazar.org)) of Johns Hopkins University and the Australian National University. I co-organised [the LSE Choice Group](https://www.lse.ac.uk/philosophy/choice-group/) on decision theory and social choice for three years. I review for *[Synthese](https://link.springer.com/journal/11229)*, *[Philosophy, Politics & Economics](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/PPE)*, *[Inquiry](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/sinq20)*, *[ACM FAccT](https://facctconference.org)*, and *[AAAI/ACM AIES](https://www.aies-conference.com/2026/)* (PC). I received the 2025 [Jacobsen Essay Prize](https://philosophy.sas.ac.uk/centres-partnerships/jacobsen-fund) of the University of London Colleges.

I received an LL.B. Philosophy, Politics and Economics from [中国人民大学 Renmin University of China](https://en.ruc.edu.cn/) where [周濂 Zhou Lian](http://ppe.ruc.edu.cn/jxtd/qzjs/axyfl/zxy/3c20e8eab82a4aac9ad187c84e59dd56.htm) supervised my bachelor dissertation and an [MSc Philosophy and Public Policy](https://www.lse.ac.uk/study-at-lse/graduate/msc-philosophy-and-public-policy#) from LSE where [Alex Voorhoeve](https://personal.lse.ac.uk/voorhoev/) supervised my master dissertation. I was a visiting PhD student at the [Australian National University](https://philosophy.cass.anu.edu.au), [University of Toronto](https://philosophy.utoronto.ca), and [Rutgers-New Brunswick](https://aristotle.rutgers.edu), sponsored respectively by [Katie Steele](https://katiesteelephilosophy.weebly.com/), [Sergio Tenenbaum](https://www.sergiotenenbaum.org/homepage.html), and [Mike Otsuka](https://sites.rutgers.edu/michael-otsuka/people/michael-otsuka/). Before starting my PhD, I worked at [Munich Re](https://www.munichre.com/en.html) for nearly a year.

I was on *Toronto Centre Podcasts* by [Toronto Centre](https://torontocentre.org), a global leader in financial regulation, to discuss how the use of AI can have significant impacts on financial regulation. Listen [here](https://www.torontocentre.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=581&Itemid=99).
