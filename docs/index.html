About me

Hi! I am a philosopher, working as an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Philosophy, the Faculty of Arts at the University of Hong Kong, since Sep 2025.

Alongside research and teaching, I work on developing and coordinating HKU's new 5-year BA & BEng in AI and Data Science double degree programme. I am an inaugural Resident Fellow at Hong Kong Global AI Governance Hub and a Research Affiliate at HKU AI & Humanity Lab.

I mainly work on practical reason and decision theory, philosophy of machine learning/artificial intelligence, philosophy of economics and finance, and moral and political philosophy more generally. Above all else, I'm exploring the intersection of decision theory and computer science. For a work I presented at the cross-disciplinary ACM FAccT 2026, see here. See the Research page for my other projects.

Education and visits

I earned my PhD in Philosophy from The London School of Economics & Political Science (LSE) in the Department of Philosophy, Logic & Scientific Method in May 2026, supervised by Campbell Brown and Kieran Oberman and previously by Johanna Thoma (Bayreuth). My thesis examiners were Richard Bradley and Ruth Chang (Oxford).

I received an LL.B. Philosophy, Politics and Economics in 2019 from 中国人民大学 Renmin University of China where 周濂 Zhou Lian supervised my bachelor dissertation and an MSc Philosophy and Public Policy in 2020 from LSE where Alex Voorhoeve supervised my master dissertation. I was a visiting PhD student at the Australian National University, University of Toronto, and Rutgers-New Brunswick, sponsored respectively by Katie Steele, Sergio Tenenbaum, and Mike Otsuka. Before starting my PhD, I worked at Munich Reinsurance for nearly a year.

Affiliations and service

I am a Research Affiliate at The Machine Intelligence and Normative Theory (MINT) Lab (PI: Seth Lazar) of Johns Hopkins University and the Australian National University. I co-organised the LSE Choice Group on decision theory and social choice for three years. I review for Synthese, Philosophy, Politics & Economics, Inquiry, ACM FAccT, and AAAI/ACM AIES (PC). I received the 2025 Jacobsen Essay Prize of the University of London Colleges.
